# BUG-002: Product Sorting Options Do Not Reorder Inventory

**Application:** SauceDemo  
**Test User:** problem_user  
**Testing Type:** Manual Testing  
**Severity:** Major  
**Priority:** High  
**Status:** Open

## Description

The inventory product sorting options do not correctly reorder the products when selected.

## Preconditions

- User is logged in as `problem_user`.
- Inventory page is displayed.

## Steps to Reproduce

1. Open https://www.saucedemo.com/
2. Login using `problem_user` and `secret_sauce`.
3. Open the sorting dropdown.
4. Select Name (Z to A).
5. Observe the product order.
6. Select Price (low to high).
7. Observe the product order.
8. Select Price (high to low).
9. Observe the product order.

## Expected Result

Products should be reordered correctly based on the selected sorting option:

- Name (Z to A): product names sorted in descending alphabetical order.
- Price (low to high): products sorted from lowest price to highest price.
- Price (high to low): products sorted from highest price to lowest price.

## Actual Result

Selecting the sorting options does not change the product order. The products remain in the same order.
