# BUG-001: Incorrect Product Details Displayed When Opening Products

**Application:** SauceDemo  
**Test User:** problem_user  
**Testing Type:** Manual Testing  
**Severity:** Major  
**Priority:** High  
**Status:** Open

## Description

When clicking product names on the inventory page, incorrect product details are displayed. The product detail page does not match the product selected from the inventory.

## Preconditions

- User is logged in as `problem_user`.
- Inventory page is displayed.

## Steps to Reproduce

1. Open https://www.saucedemo.com/
2. Login using `problem_user` and `secret_sauce`.
3. Click a product name from the inventory page.
4. Observe the product name, image, description and price on the product details page.
5. Repeat with other products.

## Expected Result

The product details page should display the correct name, image, description and price corresponding to the selected product.

## Actual Result

Incorrect product details are displayed after selecting products. Product selection and the displayed product details do not match.

## Observed Examples

- Clicking Sauce Labs Backpack opens details for Sauce Labs Fleece Jacket.
- Clicking Sauce Labs Bike Light opens details for Sauce Labs Bolt T-Shirt.
- Clicking Sauce Labs Bolt T-Shirt opens details for Sauce Labs Onesie.
- Clicking Sauce Labs Fleece Jacket displays an Item Not Found page.
- Clicking Sauce Labs Onesie opens details for Sauce Labs T-Shirt (Red).
- Clicking Sauce Labs T-Shirt (Red) opens details for Sauce Labs Backpack.
